About Humara Pandit

The science and wisdom behind your recommendation

📜

Ancient Vedic Wisdom

Our recommendations are rooted in the Ratna Shastra — the ancient Vedic science of gemology dating back over 5,000 years. Each gemstone corresponds to one of the nine celestial bodies (Navagraha) in Jyotish astrology.

⚙️

How It Works

We analyze your birth date → zodiac sign → ruling planet → nakshatra to determine which gemstone resonates with your cosmic energy. Your life goals further refine the recommendation for maximum alignment.

🔮

The Navaratna System

The nine sacred gemstones — Ruby, Pearl, Red Coral, Emerald, Yellow Sapphire, Diamond, Blue Sapphire, Hessonite, and Cat's Eye — each channel the energy of their ruling planet to benefit the wearer.

🛡️

Planetary Compatibility

Not all gemstones work well together. Our system follows the traditional planetary friendship model — Sun-Jupiter-Mars-Moon vs Saturn-Mercury-Venus — to ensure your gemstones harmonize, not conflict.
